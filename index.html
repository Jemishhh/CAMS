P/E Method vs DCF Method — Target Price (₹)

These two methods can diverge a lot, and that's informative, not a bug: the P/E method assumes CAMS keeps trading near today's ~40-43x multiple as it grows. The DCF assumes the multiple normalizes toward a steady-state level implied by WACC minus terminal growth (typically far lower). If you believe CAMS stays a "growth stock" for judges, lean P/E; if you believe multiples mean-revert, DCF is the more conservative read.

Methodology & assumptions (click to expand)

Base year (FY26A, consolidated, sourced): Revenue ₹1,516.25 Cr · EBITDA margin 45.2% · PAT ₹476.01 Cr · Shares outstanding 24.80 Cr · Non-MF revenue mix ~15.3% (Q4FY26 exit rate, CAMS IP) · D&A 6.5% of revenue · Other income 3.3% of revenue · Interest 0.5% of revenue · Net cash ~₹800 Cr (Screener.in).

Revenue build (simplified on purpose): MF revenuet = MF revenuet-1 × (1 + AUM growth) — i.e. CAMS's MF-linked revenue is modeled as scaling 1:1 with industry AUM growth, holding both CAMS's ~68% market share and MF yields (bps of AUM) flat. In reality yields have historically drifted down ~3%/yr and share moves a little — we cut both sliders to keep the tool easy to reason about, which means outputs run a touch more optimistic on the MF side than a full model would. Non-MF revenuet = Non-MF revenuet-1 × (1 + non-MF growth). Total = sum of both.

Profit build: EBITDA margin interpolates linearly from the FY26A actual to your FY29E slider value. PBT = EBITDA − D&A − Interest + Other Income (all held at FY26A ratios to revenue). PAT = PBT × (1 − 25% tax), tax rate fixed at the FY26A effective rate rather than a slider.

P/E method: Target price = FY29E EPS (3-yr forward) × exit P/E multiple.

DCF method: Free cash flowt = PATt + D&At − Capext, where Capex is assumed at 7% of revenue (above D&A's 6.5%, reflecting CAMS's stated ongoing "ReArch" platform investment — this specific ratio is an assumption, not a disclosed CAMS figure). Projected over a 5-year explicit window (FY27E-FY31E, standard DCF convention). Enterprise value = 5-year discounted FCF + discounted Gordon-growth terminal value from FY31E. Equity value = EV + FY26A net cash. Target price = equity value ÷ shares outstanding.

Known simplifications: no share dilution/buybacks modeled; no working-capital delta in FCF; MF yield & market-share held flat (see above); tax rate fixed, not adjustable; EBITDA-to-PAT bridge ratios held constant at FY26A levels rather than re-forecast individually.
