Methodology, Sources & Known Limitations

Full model construction (click to collapse)

Base year (FY26A). Revenue ₹1,516 Cr · EBITDA margin 44.9% · PAT margin 31.0% · EPS ₹18.90 · shares 24.80 Cr · CMP ₹760. Historical FY23A–FY25A revenue/EBITDA/PAT: ₹972/421/285 Cr, ₹1,136/505/351 Cr, ₹1,422/652/465 Cr.

The revenue identity. MF revenuet = Industry AAUMt × CAMS sharet × MF yieldt. Non-MF revenuet = ₹212 Cr × (1 + non-MF growth)t. Total revenue = the sum. Calibration: industry AAUM FY26 = ₹77.4 lakh Cr at 68.0% share gives ₹52.6 lakh Cr serviced; at a 2.478 bps MF-only yield that produces ₹1,304 Cr of MF revenue plus ₹212 Cr non-MF = ₹1,516 Cr, matching the actual. Run at house-case settings the model outputs FY27E–29E revenue of ₹1,714 / 1,937 / 2,188 Cr against the reference model's ₹1,713 / 1,936 / 2,188 Cr — a reconciliation within 0.2%. Market-share drift, yield change and non-MF growth are set per scenario (Bear/Base/Bull) rather than exposed as sliders; the values used are listed below.

Scenario settings. Share drift: Bear −0.75 pp/yr · Base flat · Bull +0.30 pp/yr. MF yield change: Bear −0.110 · Base −0.060 · Bull −0.030 bps/yr (observed run-rate ≈ −0.065). Non-MF growth: Bear 8% · Base 18% · Bull 25%.

Margins and earnings. EBITDA margin is straight-lined from the FY26 actual 44.9% to your FY29E exit margin. PAT margin = EBITDA margin − 13.9pp, a gap that holds within ±0.4pp across every actual and estimate year available — so EBITDA is derived rather than independently assumed. PATt = revenuet × PAT margint; EPSt = PATt ÷ 24.80 Cr shares; DPSt = EPSt × payout.

Target price and IRR. Target = EPSFY29E × exit P/E. IRR solves −CMP + DPS27/(1+r) + DPS28/(1+r)² + (DPS29 + target)/(1+r)³ = 0 by bisection — a total return including dividends, not a price-upside number.

Drawdown stress. Applies a one-off multiplicative haircut to serviced AAUM in FY28 which then compounds forward from the reduced base — i.e. the market does not "catch up". Revenue, margins, EPS, dividends, target price and IRR all recompute through the same chain.

Blending. A straight 25/50/25 probability weighting of the Bear/Base/Bull outputs (EPS, revenue, PAT, DPS, target price and IRR each weighted individually) — not a re-derivation from a separately blended cash-flow stream.

Known simplifications — stated so they can be challenged rather than discovered. (1) No share dilution or buyback is modelled. (2) The EBITDA–PAT gap is held constant, so tax-rate and other-income changes are not modelled separately. (3) Non-MF revenue is a single blended block rather than seven separately-modelled businesses. (4) Market-share drift is linear, not competitively modelled. (5) The drawdown stress hits AAUM but leaves the cost base unchanged, which understates the margin damage in a real bear market.
